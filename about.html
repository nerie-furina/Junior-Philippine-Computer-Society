Vision

The FEU Alabang chapter of the Junior Philippine Computer Society aims to produce Information and Communications Technology professionals that embody the school's values of fortitude, excellence, and uprightness through leadership, professionalism, ethics, creativity, and collaboration.

Mission

The FEU Alabang chapter of the Junior Philippine Computer Society aims to promote the importance, usage, and development of Information and Communications Technology and to improve the knowledge and skills of the youth in the field through providing complementary extra-curricular and co-curricular experiences that promote innovation, excellence, and social relevance.

Goals and objectives

Our vision for the upcoming academic year is to establish JPCS as one of the most active and impactful organizations within the school. We aim to deliver engaging and meaningful activities and events for the IT students of FEU Alabang, while fostering camaraderie, teamwork, and the enhancement of technical skills among all our members. Through these efforts, we strive to leave a lasting and memorable legacy for both JPCS and FEU Alabang in the coming school year.
