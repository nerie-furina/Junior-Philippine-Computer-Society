Interested? Join now!

Committee positions are open to students enrolled under the Information Technology program at FEU Alabang. JPCS membership is open to all first to fourth-year IT students, as well as students from other programs who are interested in joining.

JPCS Member Benefits:

• Membership Inclusions (JPCS Collar Pin and Exclusive ID Lace)
• Access to Events and Learning Opportunities
• Participate in Outside School Competitions
• Career Preparation
• Leadership Experience
• Networking and Connections
